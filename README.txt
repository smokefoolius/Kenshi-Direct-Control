================================================================
  DIRECT CONTROL FOR KENSHI               v1.5.0
================================================================

Full WASD movement for your selected character. Press V, move with
WASD, and your character responds instantly - overriding click-to-
move, combat AI pathing, and squad orders.

Includes an optional true FIRST-PERSON view - press P and drop
into your character's eyes with mouse-look, your own body and arms
visible as you move, fight, sneak, and heal.

NEW IN 1.5: MANUAL COMBAT. Press Z and every swing, block and dodge
is yours - timed presses on top of Kenshi's own skill system. Point
at an enemy and your character fights them; watch for the glint on
an incoming weapon and block or dodge it.

Built for precise, hands-on control during combat, retreats,
ambushes, city navigation, and immersive exploration - without
losing any of Kenshi's charm.

CONTROLS
--------
  V          Toggle Direct Control on / off
  W A S D    Move (camera-relative)
  X          Cycle speed (Walk / Jog / Run)
  G          Hand control to the character under the cursor
  Dbl-click  Switch control via a squad portrait

  P          Toggle first-person view (while Direct Control is on)
  CTRL       Toggle the third-person action camera (over-the-
             shoulder view with a centered crosshair)
  H          Swap the camera to the other shoulder
  SHIFT+C    Toggle sneak (first person or third-person camera)
  ALT        Hold for a free mouse cursor in first / third person

  Z          Manual combat on / off
  E          Attack  - one press, one swing at the enemy you point at
  F          Block   - hold to guard; tap as a hit lands for a
             perfect block
  Q          Dodge   - press as an attack comes in
  Right-click an enemy (in combat) to make them your target at any
  distance.

  Every key can be changed in-game: Options > Direct Control.


FEATURES
--------
  Instant movement override
    WASD immediately cancels click-to-move destinations and
    overrides combat AI pathing. No startup delay, crisp stop on
    release, indoors and out. Movement, speed and camera now carry
    straight through the loading of new map areas.

  Manual combat   (press Z)   NEW
    Turn it on and your character stops swinging on their own.
    Every attack is your Attack key, with the game picking the
    technique for your weapon and skill exactly as it would for the
    AI. Out of reach? Your character steps in and swings when they
    get there. Press Attack during a dodge and the swing fires the
    instant the dodge ends.

    Block works two ways. HOLD it and your character guards using
    their Block skill, with the game's own +20 defence bonus, for
    up to about a second before the guard drops (no endless
    turtling). TAP it just as a hit lands and it is a perfect block:
    stopped no matter your skill, with the parry sound and sparks -
    and if the enemy's technique strikes twice, both strikes are
    covered. A perfect block needs the hit to come from the front,
    and it pays you back: your weapon glows blue, and while it does
    the next Block press counts straight away with no cooldown - so
    a run of well-timed taps can stop a whole flurry.

    Dodge is a timed press: hit it as an attack comes in and your
    character performs one of the game's dodge animations. Whether
    it succeeds is a Dodge skill roll against the attacker, with a
    bonus for the timing, so a novice still gets clipped and a
    master slips through. Dodges work from any direction.

    Getting hit matters. A stagger plays through and nothing you
    press during it registers - no cancelling out of a knockback.
    Accidental double-presses are forgiven: a second tap never
    restarts a move, and letting go of Block for an instant keeps
    the same guard.

    By default your character still blocks on their own while
    manual combat is on, and your key presses add to that. Prefer
    a fully manual defence? Turn "Character still blocks on their
    own" off in the settings and only your presses stop hits.

  Aim focus   NEW
    While manual combat is on, your character fights whoever you
    point at: the enemy under the mouse cursor in the normal
    camera, or the one nearest the crosshair in first person and
    the third-person camera. The camera is never moved for it. The
    focus sticks until you point at someone else, and a right-click
    on an enemy in combat picks them out at any distance. The
    targeted enemy shows their name in red above their head, with
    thin bars for blood, head, chest and stomach so you can see how
    the fight is going. Not pointing at anyone? The name and bars
    follow whoever your character is fighting. They step out of the
    way when the enemy says something.

  Weapon glint   NEW
    The moment an enemy starts a swing at you, their weapon begins
    to glow, brightest just before the blow lands. That is your cue
    to block, dodge or counter. Works in every camera view.

  First-person view   (press P)
    While Direct Control is on, press P to drop into a true first-
    person camera at your character's eyes. Look around with the
    mouse and move with WASD; your own body and arms stay in view
    as you run, fight, block, and heal for a full first-person
    presence. The camera turns your body to face where you look,
    and the mouse frees itself automatically for the map, dialogue,
    inventory, and menus - or hold ALT for the cursor any time.
    Press P again to return to the normal view. Distant grass shimmers far less when you turn, and when
    you are knocked down or thrown, you see your own body in front
    of you rather than through it.

  Sneak in first person and third person   (SHIFT+C)
    Toggle sneaking without leaving the view - it presses the
    game's own SNEAK button, so the UI stays in sync and stealth
    skill, detection, and XP all work exactly as vanilla.

  A combat camera that stays clean
    The view keeps clear of the action: your own swings, blocks,
    and heals never clip through the lens, the camera eases back
    off the steps when climbing stairs, and an enemy pressing into
    your face can't slice the view open - while your arms and
    weapon stay visible throughout.

  Combat that gets out of your way
    With manual combat off, stand still and the AI fights on its
    own - block, dodge, attack, earning XP as normal. Hold WASD and
    it yields so movement stays smooth. A committed animation (your
    swing, a stagger, a parry) always finishes before you move.

  Move while you loot and trade   (optional)
    Turn the face-cam off (see CONFIG) to keep moving with WASD
    while an inventory or trade window is open, camera locked on
    your character. In first person and the third-person camera the
    mouse keeps looking around; hold ALT for the cursor to move
    items. Walk away from a merchant and the trade closes on its
    own. Talking to NPCs still pauses as normal.

  Inventory face-cam   (on by default)
    Open a character's own inventory and the camera swings to face
    them so you can see equipped gear. Auto-disabled in combat so
    you can loot and disarm enemies freely.

  Get up and go
    Hold WASD while sitting, lying in a bed, or operating a
    workstation and your character stands up and walks off.

  Third-person action camera   (press CTRL)
    CTRL swings the camera in behind your character's shoulder
    with the crosshair locked to the center of the screen - mouse
    to look, WASD to move, scroll wheel to zoom. Press H to swap
    to the other shoulder. The camera eases clear of walls behind
    you and ignores your own gear. Menus and pause free the cursor
    automatically and the view re-locks when they close. When the
    crosshair rests on something you can interact with - an enemy,
    a door, loot, a conversation - the familiar cursor icon appears
    in its place (works in first person too). Press CTRL again to
    return to the normal camera.

  In-game settings
    Everything is configurable from inside the game: open Options
    and find the "Direct Control" tab. Keys first, then settings
    grouped by mode - movement, manual combat, third person, first
    person - each with a plain description. Changes apply instantly
    and save on their own. (The config file still works if you
    prefer editing text.)

  Per-character control
    Direct Control follows your selected character; switch any time
    with G or a double-click. If your only conscious character is
    knocked out, Direct Control switches itself off; press V again
    once they wake.

CONFIG  (WASDCombatPlugin.ini in the mod folder)
------------------------------------------------
  Everything below is easier to change in-game: Options > Direct
  Control. The file is for hand-editing.

  [Keybinds]
    Rebind any key here. Valid names: letters (W), digits (5),
    F1..F24, SPACE, TAB, SHIFT, CONTROL, arrow keys, NUMPAD0..9,
    or OEM_1..8 for international layouts. Bad entries fall back
    to the default. Sneak is always SHIFT + the SneakToggle key.
    New in 1.5: Block = F, Dodge = Q, Attack = E, ManualAttack = Z,
    and SelectControl moves from F to G. Leave KeyLayout = 2 alone -
    it stops the one-time key update from running again.

  [Settings]
    InventoryFaceCam = true    Camera faces your character on
                               inventory (false = keep moving).
    WasdSpeedCap     = true    Cap WASD speed at the character's
                               real max (injuries, loads, shackles).
    WasdSpeedMult    = 1.0     Scales WASD movement speed.
    EnemyPursuit     = true    Enemies chase and hit a moving player.

    ManualBlock      = true    Block and Dodge keys on/off.
    AiBlocksInManual = true    Character still blocks on their own
                               in manual combat (false = only your
                               presses stop hits).
    BlockHoldMs      = 1200    Longest a held Block guards for.
    BlockCooldownMs  = 800     Wait before Block counts again.
    PerfectBlockWindowMs   = 1000   Perfect-block window after a tap.
    PerfectBlockRewardMs   = 1200   After a perfect block, how long the
                                    next press is free (blue glow).
    DodgeWindowMs    = 600     How long a Dodge press is remembered.
    DodgeSkillBonus  = 20      Dodge skill added for a timed press.
    DodgeCooldownMs  = 300     Extra wait after a dodge.
    AttackWindowMs   = 400     How long an Attack press is remembered.
    AttackApproachMs = 1500    Step-in time for an out-of-reach attack.
    AttackRecoveryMs = 400     Pause after a swing before the next
    DodgeRecoveryMs  = 400     Attack / Dodge press counts.
    InputRepeatGuardMs = 250   Ignore double-presses closer than this.
    AimConeDeg       = 35      Crosshair target cone (first / third
                               person).
    AimHoverPx       = 70      Cursor target radius (normal camera).
    AimNameTag       = true    Name and health bars over the target.
    WeaponGlint      = true    Glint on weapons swinging at you.
    GlintLeadMs      = 700     Glint peaks this long after the swing
                               starts.
    GlintPx          = 22      Glint size.

    OtsDistance = 20, OtsHeight = 15.0, OtsSide = 4.0, OtsNearClip = 0.15
                               Third-person camera framing.
    VerboseLog       = false   Detailed log for bug reports.

  [FirstPerson]
    Sensitivity      = 1.0     Mouse-look speed.
    FOV              = 75      Field of view (50-110).
    NearClip         = 1.0     Clipping distance: lower shows more
                               of your body but shimmers distant
                               grass when you turn.
    DownNearClip     = 0.05    Clipping distance while knocked down.
    BodySolidInside  = 1       Your body is solid from the inside.
    HeadPixelHide    = 1       Hide your own head (per-pixel).
    HideHead         = 0       Legacy head hide for custom races.
    HideHeadgear     = 1       Hide hats, helmets, masks, beard.
    HideHair         = 1       Hide your own hair.
    HideInjuryOverlay = 1      Suppress hand-to-wound poses.
    FloorRevealBelow = 1       Render the storeys below you.

    Every other camera value (eye level, forward position, and
    more) is tuned automatically; the advanced sliders are at the
    bottom of the settings tab.


INSTALLATION
------------
  1. Install RE_Kenshi (required mod loader).
  2. Copy the "WASDCombatPlugin" folder into:  Kenshi/mods/
     so you have:  Kenshi/mods/WASDCombatPlugin/WASDCombatPlugin.dll
     (keep the included "materials" subfolder - it carries the
     first-person head-hiding shader).
  3. Launch the game. RE_Kenshi loads the plugin automatically.
  4. Enable ONLY ONE copy of Direct Control. If you have both the
     Workshop item and a manual install, untick one - the mod
     refuses to load twice and says so in RE_Kenshi_log.txt.

COMPATIBILITY & NOTES
---------------------
  - Tested with RE_Kenshi 0.3.5 (Kenshi 1.0.65). No game files
    are modified on disk, so it is safe alongside large mod lists
    and most combat, AI, and faction mods.
  - The mod includes a copy of the character shader
    (materials/deferred/character.hlsl) with a small addition that
    enables the first-person head hiding. It behaves identically
    to vanilla outside first person. A mod that replaces the same
    shader file may conflict - whichever loads last wins. If
    another mod's copy wins, the plugin detects it and falls back
    to the legacy head shrink.
  - CHARACTER HIGHLIGHT: that mod also replaces character.hlsl.
    Install the separate "Character Highlight Compatibility Patch"
    download, enable it and put it LAST in your load order, below
    both mods.
  - Manual combat uses the game's own techniques, skill rolls and
    XP; nothing is added to your character's numbers except the
    timed-press dodge bonus (DodgeSkillBonus) and the game's own
    block-mode defence bonus while you hold Block.
  - A perfect block only covers hits from the front. Dodges cover
    every direction.
  - Direct Control drives one character at a time - the selected
    one. Other squad members follow their normal orders.
  - With manual combat off you cannot attack while actively moving
    - stop, and the AI fights on its own.
  - During knockdown, stagger, or get-up animations the character
    cannot be moved; movement resumes when the animation completes.
  - Downed or crippled characters can still be moved using the
    game's crawl / limp system.
  - While manning a turret, aiming stays vanilla until you press
    WASD, which steps you off the turret.
  - First person: the clipping distance is a trade-off between
    seeing your own chest and a calmer horizon. 1.0 is the
    middle; it is a slider in the settings.
  - Known minor issue: if a character walks off on their own
    orders the instant their inventory closes, the camera can lag
    behind for a moment before catching up (any input snaps it
    back).
